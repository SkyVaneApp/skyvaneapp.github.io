English

GlibberJagd collects no personal data: no account, no analytics, no ads, no in-app purchases, no server of its own. The camera image (room, hand and road detection), the front camera during breaks (counting exercises) and the motion sensors (is the phone standing still) are processed on device only while in use and are never stored or sent. First names, school grades and favourite monsters of the players, history, scores and aliens built in the lab stay on the device. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
